# Revision Response Template (R→A→C Format)

This template helps authors structure their response to reviewers. The R→A→C format (Reviewer comment → Author response → Changes made) is the standard expected by most venues.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. Copy each reviewer comment from the Revision Roadmap
2. Write your response addressing the concern directly
3. Specify exactly where in the manuscript you made changes
4. If you disagree with a comment, explain why respectfully and provide evidence

## Checklist

Before submitting the response:
- [ ] All Priority 1 items have detailed responses with manuscript changes
- [ ] All Priority 2 items have responses (changes or justified no-change)
- [ ] Priority 3 items are at least acknowledged
- [ ] All DA-CRITICAL items are thoroughly addressed
- [ ] Changed text is highlighted in the manuscript
- [ ] Page/section references are accurate for the revised manuscript
- [ ] Response is professional and constructive in tone

## Tips for Effective Responses

### Do:
- Thank reviewers for specific helpful comments
- Be direct and concise in responses
- Provide evidence (new experiments, citations, analysis) when disagreeing
- Reference exact locations of changes (page, section, paragraph, line if possible)
- Address the spirit of the comment, not just the letter

### Don't:
- Be defensive or dismissive
- Ignore difficult comments
- Make changes without explaining them in the response
- Promise future work as a substitute for addressing current concerns
- Respond to reviewers differently based on their tone (always be professional)

### When You Disagree:
It's acceptable to respectfully disagree with a reviewer. Structure your disagreement as:
1. Acknowledge the reviewer's concern: "We understand the reviewer's concern that..."
2. Present your reasoning: "However, we believe..."
3. Provide evidence: "This is supported by [citation / experiment / analysis]..."
4. Offer a compromise if possible: "To address this concern, we have added [X] to the manuscript while maintaining [Y]..."
